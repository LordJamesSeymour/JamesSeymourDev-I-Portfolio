# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Game-studio recruiters, hiring managers, technical leads, and collaborators evaluating James
Seymour's game programming, engine, networking, VR, and level-design work. They need to understand
the strongest projects quickly, then open readable case studies for technical depth.

## Product Purpose

Present James's work as a premium, projects-first professional portfolio. The site should create a
memorable studio-grade first impression while remaining fast, clear, data-driven, editable, and
deployable as a static GitHub Pages site.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, technical, immersive, confident, and intentional. The experience should feel like a
high-end game-studio presentation without becoming a game itself.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI or SaaS landing-page patterns, bright white portfolio templates, rainbow gradients,
playful or cartoonish styling, cluttered low-contrast layouts, scroll hijacking, and experiences that
hide essential project information behind WebGL or complex interaction.

## Design Principles

- Put real project work and proof ahead of decoration.
- Use immersion as progressive enhancement; the readable DOM portfolio is always complete.
- Make the first scan fast for recruiters, then support deeper technical reading.
- Preserve one coherent cinematic identity as new media and projects are added.
- Keep content and media data-driven so the portfolio remains easy to maintain.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast for text, preserve visible keyboard focus and semantic controls, provide useful
alternative text and iframe titles, respect reduced-motion preferences, and retain complete 2D
fallbacks when video, WebGL, or external embeds are unavailable.
